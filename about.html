Our Mission

In the heart of West Africa, Abidjan stands as a testament to human resilience and innovation. This blog chronicles the remarkable stories of communities, individuals, and organizations working towards environmental sustainability and energy access in Côte d'Ivoire's economic capital.

Through in-depth reporting and community voices, we explore how Abidjan is navigating the challenges of rapid urbanization, climate change, and energy transition.

What We Cover

Environmental Sustainability

Community Resilience

Hey, I'm here

As a journalist and environmental advocate based in Abidjan, I've witnessed firsthand the incredible resilience of our communities. This platform is my passion - a way to amplify the voices of everyday heroes making a difference in our city.

Through these stories, I hope to inspire action, foster dialogue, and contribute to the ongoing transformation of Abidjan into a more sustainable and equitable city.
